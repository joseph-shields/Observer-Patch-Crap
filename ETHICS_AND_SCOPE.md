# Ethics, scope and non-harassment policy

This is a technical and provenance-neutral audit.

- Critique claims, dependency structures, commits and public artifacts; do not insult or harass individuals.
- Do not contact employers, family members or unrelated third parties.
- Do not publish private messages, personal addresses, private email headers or unredacted personal evidence in this repository.
- Do not allege criminal conduct, fraud, theft or legal plagiarism without an appropriate independent process.
- Preserve contrary evidence and documented antecedents.
- Correct errors publicly and version every amendment.
- Separate mathematical invalidity, physical insufficiency, conceptual ancestry and subjective intent.

The strongest claim made here is that source-only physical closure fails and that a documented sequence warrants a public conceptual-ancestry explanation. Internal mathematical theorems may remain valid in their declared scope.
