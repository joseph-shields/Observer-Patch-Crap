# Evidence ledger and claim boundary

## Evidentiary claim

The public record supports a serious question about **functional architectural convergence**: after a directly adverse source-to-physics audit, OPH rapidly adopted dynamics-first action reconstruction, information/history-first least action, premise ancestry, claim-status ledgers, no-privilege candidate rules, countermodels, record/readout closure, explicit physical-attachment gates, observer-clock distinctions, and open-row/no-promotion language.

## What this package does not allege

- It does not allege theft, plagiarism, fraud or subjective intent.
- It does not claim ownership of standard mathematics or physics.
- It does not use generic Born, Lorentz, Yukawa or golden-ratio overlap as standalone evidence.
- It does not publish private messages or identify the prior-corpus author.
- It does not claim that internal OPH theorems are false merely because physical closure fails.

## The technical claim

The source-only OPH architecture does not entail physical instantiation, a unique boundary quantum theory or a unique physical bulk. The Lean kernel proves generic non-definability and theorem-volume results. The Mr Tickle Screen is a reductio against semantic promotion, not a claim about artwork.

## The ancestry request

For each post-audit architecture listed in the ledger, provide a dated pre-contact development trail or state whether the prior corpus or supplied audit influenced the research or AI context. If it did, identify and attribute the influence. Independent implementation of code and local proofs does not by itself answer conceptual ancestry of the selected dependency recipe.

## Public evidence classes

1. **A - directly provable:** commit dates, messages, files, hashes and public status changes.
2. **B - strong structural inference:** same functional move, ordering and role after the prior dated architecture.
3. **C - contextual convergence:** meaningful but generic or partly pre-existing.
4. **X - excluded control:** documented OPH antecedent; not used as a provenance allegation.
