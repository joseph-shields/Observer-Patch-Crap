This repository does not allege theft, plagiarism or intent. It asks a narrower question about the ancestry of a rapidly converging dependency architecture.

## AI-context question

If an AI system generated or materially shaped the relevant commits, identify the files, conversations, prompts, external audits or prior-corpus materials supplied to that system. Independent generation by an AI does not resolve provenance if prior material was in context.

## Attribution standard

Attribution is appropriate when another person's audit or architecture materially influenced the selection, ordering, explanatory role or scientific governance of later work, even where no sentence or code fragment was copied verbatim.

## Claim boundary

A refusal or absence of disclosure is not proof of copying. It leaves conceptual ancestry unresolved.
