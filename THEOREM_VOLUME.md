# Why theorem volume contributes zero physical-selection information

Let `U : Phys -> Src` forget the physical lift. A judgement `tau : Phys -> Bool` factors through the source when `tau = tauBar o U` for some `tauBar`. If `U(P)=U(Q)`, every source-factorized judgement has `tau(P)=tau(Q)`.

For a finite family define `F_N(X)=(tau_1(X),...,tau_N(X))`. Then `F_N(P)=F_N(Q)` for every finite `N`, the discrimination count is zero, and under an equal binary prior the mutual information `I(lift;F_N)=0 bits`.

This does not make internal theorems false. It proves that their evidential contribution to the unresolved physical-lift choice is zero. Conversely, a theorem that separates the lifts cannot factor through the common source reduct and therefore consumes additional load-bearing structure.
