# Holography references used by the audit

The paper uses these references for a narrow typing claim: **a bare finite incidence screen is not the input object of standard holographic reconstruction.** Standard reconstruction begins with a specified boundary quantum theory (and a duality/reconstruction framework), not with the number of boundary points alone.

## Maldacena - AdS/CFT

Juan Maldacena, *The Large N Limit of Superconformal Field Theories and Supergravity* (1997/1998).

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200
```

Relevance: the correspondence relates a specific large-N conformal field theory to a specific string/gravitational description. The boundary theory is not supplied merely by a boundary cellulation.

## Witten / GKPW formulation

Edward Witten, *Anti de Sitter Space And Holography* (1998).

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9802150
```

Relevance: boundary generating functionals and bulk gravitational boundary data are related through a concrete dictionary. Again, the dictionary consumes specified theory/dynamics.

## HKLL bulk reconstruction

Alex Hamilton, Daniel Kabat, Gilad Lifschytz and David A. Lowe, *Local bulk operators in AdS/CFT: a boundary view of horizons and locality* (2006; arXiv 2005).

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0506118
```

Schematic form used in the paper:

```text
phi_bulk(X) = integral K(X|x) O_boundary(x) dx.
```

The audit does **not** claim that HKLL proves ontological reality. The point is that HKLL reconstructs bulk operators from operators of an already specified boundary quantum theory within a semiclassical AdS/CFT setting. A twelve-point drawing does not provide the operator algebra, state/code sector, Hamiltonian, correlators, bulk equations, or smearing kernel.

## Quantum-error-correcting interpretation

Ahmed Almheiri, Xi Dong and Daniel Harlow, *Bulk Locality and Quantum Error Correction in AdS/CFT* (2015).

```text
https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7041
```

Relevance: the bulk/boundary map has encoding/reconstruction structure. An encoding map presupposes the logical/boundary structures it relates; bare incidence alone is not the code.

## The typing claim actually used

The paper needs only:

```text
BareScreen != BoundaryQuantumTheory
BoundaryQuantumTheory + Dictionary -> BulkReconstruction
```

and the elementary factorization obstruction:

```text
same bare screen
+ two inequivalent boundary theories
+ different bulk/readout outputs
----------------------------------
no unique bulk map factors through the bare screen alone.
```

This conclusion does not require taking a position on the completeness of AdS/CFT, de Sitter holography, or the ontology of bulk versus boundary descriptions.
