## Question

Can the frozen OPH source theory uniquely determine a physically instantiated world, including a physical boundary theory and bulk reconstruction, **without** importing an independently grounded realization relation, Hamiltonian/dynamics, calibration, code subspace or holographic dictionary?

## Existing obstruction

The included Lean kernel proves four generic results.

1. **No Semantic Alchemy.** A satisfiable source theory cannot force an unconstrained external physical-realization relation to be inhabited. Any source-only enrichment still admits an empty realization expansion.
2. **Public-quotient obstruction.** If two physical states share the public/source quotient but a physical readout distinguishes them, that readout cannot factor through the quotient.
3. **No bulk from bare screen.** If two boundary theories share a bare screen but yield different correlations or bulk reconstructions, no boundary/bulk assignment factors through the screen alone.
4. **Theorem-volume null result.** Any finite family of source-factorized judgements has identical truth fingerprints on same-reduct lifts and zero discrimination information about the realized physical lift.

## Required successful response

A successful closure must provide all of the following:

1. an exact source-defined nonempty physical realization relation, not an internal predicate merely named `physical`;
2. a physical boundary theory: observable algebra, state/code subspace, dynamics/Hamiltonian, correlations and calibration;
3. a reconstruction map to bulk observables with a declared domain and validity regime;
4. proof that all admissible realizations are observationally equivalent, or a source-selected unique realization;
5. proof that every physical readout claimed by OPH factors through the public/source quotient;
6. identification of a specific frozen premise violated by the featureless and Mr Tickle witnesses;
7. exact ancestry and versioning of every additional load-bearing object.

## Non-solutions

Adding source-only theorems, renaming a relation `physical`, appealing to intended meaning, treating consensus as ontological creation, invoking theorem count, naming an underived interface, excluding a separating experiment, or projecting a later premise backward do not solve the problem.

## Resolution outcomes

The problem closes only if the source-defined physical closure is constructed and checked; the additional physical premises are registered and the public claim narrowed; OPH accepts the scope `abstract/conditional reconstruction`; or a concrete error is found in a theorem, witness or evidence row here.
